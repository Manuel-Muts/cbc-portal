Privacy Policy

A clear guide to how CBE Portal handles the information that helps schools teach, manage, and support learners.

Last reviewed
September 2026
Your information mattersWe keep this page simple so staff can quickly understand the principles behind the portal’s data handling.

Information we use

We use account, school, learner, academic, and payment information supplied by authorized school users to operate the portal.

How information is protected

Access is controlled by authenticated accounts and role-based permissions. Schools are responsible for keeping account credentials private.

Data retention

Information is retained as needed for school records, reporting, support, security, and legal obligations.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact your school administrator or the CBC Portal support team.
