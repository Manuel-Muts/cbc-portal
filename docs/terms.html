Terms of Service

The practical principles that keep CBE Portal useful, secure, and accountable for every school community.

Last reviewed
September 2026
Use with careThese terms set expectations for authorized users and help protect the school records entrusted to the platform.

Authorized use

Users must use only the access granted to their role, keep credentials secure, and enter accurate information.

School responsibility

Each school is responsible for managing users, protecting learner information, and reviewing records submitted through its accounts.

Service availability

We work to keep the portal reliable, but maintenance, updates, network failures, or circumstances outside our control may temporarily affect availability.

Support

For account or service questions, contact your school administrator or the CBC Portal support team.
